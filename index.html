The pledge, in plain words

We built PageRain to be useful.
We're running it to be good.

PageRain is a small tool with modest subscription revenue. We don't need it to make us rich — so we're not going to let it. Here is exactly what we commit to, and exactly what we hold back.

✓

What we give

100% of net profit in the 2026 and 2027 calendar years — every dollar left after the real cost of running PageRain — is donated to charities working on human rights.

From 2028 onward, at least 80% of net profit will be donated every year. This is an open-ended commitment with no expiry date.

↺

What we hold back

Before there's any profit to give, revenue first covers operational expenses — servers, platform API fees, payment processing, and paying the people who keep it running.

We also keep a small operating reserve so a slow month can't take the service — or the pledge — down. That reserve is capped.

Net profit = subscription & usage revenue − operating costs − a capped reserve − any unrecovered losses from earlier periods. We donate 100% of that net profit in 2026–2027 and never less than 80% in any year after. If PageRain runs at break-even or at a loss, the pledge gives $0 for that period. Giving resumes only after the accumulated loss has been fully recovered and PageRain is generating net profit again.

Where it lands

Causes we give to.

We give to established, independently-rated organisations defending human rights — spread across the areas below so no single quarter is a lottery for one cause.

Civil & political rights

Free expression, fair trials, protection from arbitrary detention and censorship.

Refugees & the displaced

Legal aid, resettlement support, and emergency relief for people forced from home.

Press & digital freedom

Protecting journalists, whistleblowers, and open access to information online.

Equality & anti-discrimination

Defending the rights of women, minorities, and LGBTQ+ people worldwide.

Human-rights defenders

Direct support and protection for activists and lawyers who put themselves at risk.

Accountability & justice

Documenting abuses and pursuing justice for victims through credible legal channels.
